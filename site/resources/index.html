Quick answers to common questions. For complete guidance, consult the full FAQ and your association's governing documents.

Read the full FAQ ↗
What does an HOA management company do?+

It supports the day-to-day work of a community association, including finances, record-keeping, maintenance coordination, rule compliance, and communication with homeowners and boards.

What's the difference between the board and management?+

The elected HOA board sets direction and policy. The management company handles many operational and administrative tasks under the board's oversight.

Where does Association Services BCS operate?+

The office is in College Station and serves community associations throughout Bryan–College Station and up to approximately 55 miles beyond, according to the current FAQ.

How do I request an exterior modification?+

Use the architectural request form and follow your community's approval requirements before beginning exterior changes.

Where can I find governing documents?+

Check the official community directory for association-specific governing documents and available resources, or request help through the office.
