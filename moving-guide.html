BUILD MY MOVING CHECKLIST ↗

The best moving day isn't the one where you work the hardest. It's the one where everybody knows the plan. Here's how to get your home, your belongings, and the people helping you ready for the move.

01 / 4–6 WEEKS OUT

GET THE GAME PLAN.

  • Confirm the move date and get moving estimates early.
  • Walk every room and identify what will be moved, sold, donated, or recycled.
  • List unusually heavy or awkward pieces and anything that needs disassembly.
  • Find out whether your new address has access, parking, stairs, or elevator requirements.
  • Make one shared folder for contracts, schedules, utility information, and addresses.
02 / 2–3 WEEKS OUT

PACK SMARTER.

  • Start with seasonal items, storage spaces, and rooms you use less often.
  • Use small boxes for heavy items such as books, and sturdier boxes for fragile items.
  • Mark the destination room on multiple sides of every box.
  • Photograph cable connections before disconnecting electronics.
  • Ask your movers how they want fragile, oversized, and specialty items handled.
03 / THE WEEK BEFORE

REMOVE SURPRISES.

  • Confirm the scheduled date, addresses, and your best contact number with your mover.
  • Arrange utility transitions and update mailing addresses.
  • Keep medication, important documents, keys, chargers, and daily essentials separate.
  • Confirm elevator reservations and any special loading rules.
  • Plan for children and pets to be somewhere safe during loading.
04 / MOVING DAY

LET'S GET MOVING.

  • Keep clear, safe walkways from rooms to the loading area.
  • Point out fragile, bulky, or high-priority items to the moving crew.
  • Keep valuables and essentials with you unless another arrangement has been made.
  • Do a final walkthrough to check closets, cabinets, storage spaces, and the garage.
  • At the destination, direct boxes to the right rooms as they come in.
