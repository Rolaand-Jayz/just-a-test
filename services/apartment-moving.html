HOW WE HELP

SMALLER FLOOR PLAN. STILL A BIG MOVE.

Apartments, condos, and multi-story buildings can be harder to move than they look. Long walks from the parking lot, awkward corners, stairs, and limited loading space all make a difference. Rescue 1 helps people handle those moving-day obstacles without turning them into a crisis.

If you are moving into or out of an Amarillo-area apartment, tell us about your building access, floor, elevator availability, and parking arrangements when you request a quote. A little advance planning goes a long way toward making the day move smoothly.

  • Moves into and out of apartments and multi-story buildings
  • Help with large furniture and tight spaces
  • Planning around stairs, elevators, and loading access
  • Packing and moving labor options on request
