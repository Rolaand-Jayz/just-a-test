HOW WE HELP

EVERY BOX HAS A STORY. EVERY HOME DESERVES CARE.

A home move is more than a truckload of boxes. It is everything you have built, collected, and cared for—heading somewhere new. Whether you are changing neighborhoods in Amarillo, moving into your first home, or making a bigger change across the Panhandle, Rescue 1 Movers works to make the physical part simpler.

Our firefighter-owned team brings a service-first mindset to your move. We help with the hard parts: heavy furniture, rooms full of belongings, careful loading, and getting everything to the next stop. Request a free estimate with your addresses, timing, and any oversized items so the crew can discuss what you need.

  • Moving help for houses and townhomes
  • Careful handling of furniture and household belongings
  • Local and long-distance moving discussions
  • Packing, loading and unloading help available on request
