    What kinds of moves do you handle? +

    We help with residential, apartment, office, local, and long-distance moves. Packing, loading, unloading, and furniture assembly help may also be available. Tell us about the job when requesting your estimate.

    Do you offer free moving estimates? +

    Yes. Use the estimate form on this page or call (806) 678-6972. We'll work from the details of your move so you can discuss the right services for your situation.

    Are you insured? +

    Rescue 1 Movers identifies itself as fully insured. Ask the team directly about the specific coverage and valuation options applicable to your move.

    Do you move outside Amarillo? +

    Yes. Rescue 1 handles moves around Amarillo and the Texas Panhandle, and can discuss long-distance moves. Contact the crew with your origin, destination, and timeline to confirm availability.

    Can you help if I only need extra hands? +

    Absolutely worth asking. The company advertises packing and labor help, including loading, unloading, assembly, and disassembly. Describe the work you need in your request.

    When should I reach out about my move? +

    As soon as you know your preferred date. Availability varies, so the earlier you contact Rescue 1, the more time you'll have to discuss the move, access details, and any special handling needs.
